# T-12 disposable probes

`notify-blocked`: authorized disposable topic/egress and blocked Ticket → received blocked notification. `notify-doctor`: automatic Doctor issue → received Doctor notification. `notify-absent`: no topic → transition unchanged/no send. `notify-unavailable-blocked`, `notify-unavailable-doctor`: controlled unavailable notifier → each transition still completes. `notify-completion-drift`: completion → public promised event observed or failed documentation-contract assertion.

Evidence and recovery: Pre-run topic authority, nonsecret received payload/identity/time and lifecycle logs. Undocumented review/rate-limit/events filtering are not mandatory support.
