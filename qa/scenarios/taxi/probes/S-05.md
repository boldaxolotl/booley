# S-05 disposable probes

`mandatory-block`: unmet mandatory Criterion → cannot review. `optional-justify`: unmet optional Criterion with current justification → documented acceptance. `evidence-stale`: relevant edit → prior evidence unusable. `evidence-rerun`: rerun exact sealed contract → current evidence. `out-contract`: undeclared Flow/Target call → rejected. `diagnostic-call`: explicit diagnostic invocation → allowed without satisfying sealed acceptance. `fail-fix-pass`: real failing sim then fix/pass → retained pre-fix failure required and linked.

Evidence and recovery: Criterion evaluations and all report/source identities before and after; one check per observed acceptance transition.
