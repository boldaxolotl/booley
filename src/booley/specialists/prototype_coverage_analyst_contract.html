Throwaway decision prototype

Report-driven Coverage Analyst

Question: does a separately invoked, Criterion-free Specialist remain useful when it receives one persisted per-Target Coverage Campaign, may read only a fingerprint-matched source snapshot, separates measured evidence from causal hypotheses, and can recommend—but never perform—coverage closure or waiver approval?

Nothing here is production code. The Analyst never launches simulation, reads waveforms or native coverage databases, changes Coverage Points, applies or approves waivers, mutates Criteria, or decides whether a Ticket closes.

Proposed external interface

coverage_analyst( campaign: Path, # one canonical Target coverage.json instruction: str = "", ) -> CoverageAnalysisReport

The wrapper decodes the Campaign, verifies source fingerprints, and invokes one deep analysis module. There is no --target, --test, --scope, --criteria, or waiver-reset surface.

analyze_coverage_campaign( campaign: CoverageCampaign, sources: AnalystSourceSnapshot | None, instruction: str, ) -> CoverageAnalysisReport

A successful analysis exits successfully even when coverage is not ready. Only an invalid input or Specialist/infrastructure failure makes the invocation fail.

Source-access policy

Allowed: read-only RTL and testbench sources belonging to the Campaign Target, from a disposable snapshot whose aggregate fingerprints equal the Campaign.
Fallback: if sources are missing or stale, continue report-only and suppress source-derived hypotheses.
Never exposed: waveforms, raw or merged native databases, Criteria/configuration files, the approved-waiver directory, unrelated project files, shell execution, or write capabilities.

Deterministic waiver-candidate policy

Ready for human review: one exact, uncovered, eligible, V1-scored Coverage Point from a complete compatible Campaign; fingerprint-matched source supports a Target-specific excluded rationale, or an unreachable rationale carries authenticated formal-proof evidence identifying the point mapping, tool/version, proof result, environment, assumptions, and immutable artifact.
Investigation only: the idea is plausible but source is unavailable/stale, collection is incomplete, the causal account is uncertain, or an unreachability claim still needs formal proof. Confidence cannot promote it past a missing objective requirement.
Forbidden: covered, already-waived, unscored/reported-only, unknown, ambiguous, or inexact points; wildcard, line-, file-, metric-, or multi-Target exclusions; and attempts to hide collection failures, unavailable capabilities, difficult stimulus, deadlines, or a missed threshold.

The wrapper validates every model-produced candidate against these rules. The three results are ready_for_human_review, investigate, and forbidden; model confidence is recorded but never acts as approval authority.

Primary-source comparison: other systems commonly expose manual exclusion and formal-unreachability pathways, but none surveyed publishes Booley's exact closed pair. See coverage waiver and exclusion classifications in other systems.

Versioned analysis output

Evidence claims cite exact Campaign fields or Coverage Point ids. Source reading may support a hypothesis, but never upgrades it into measured evidence. A waiver candidate stays inside this report and leaves the point eligible.
