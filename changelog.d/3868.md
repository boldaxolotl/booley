- A project can declare intentional combinational loops and latches in
  `booley.toml`: `[flows.synth].expected_comb_loops`, and `expected_latches` and
  `expected_comb_loops` under `[flows.fpga]`. Each is a non-negative integer
  defaulting to 0, like `[flows.synth].expected_latches`. Only the excess over
  the declared count fails the run, and the report names the actual, expected,
  and excess counts.
