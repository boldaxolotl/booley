- Breaking: reports and `endpoint_end` events no longer carry `criterion_key` or
  `criterion_met`, and `McpToolResult` no longer takes them, so a Project endpoint
  that passes them fails. The Dashboard Job details lose the Criterion row, the
  Jobs list loses the `Crit` column, and the web Dashboard result card loses its
  Criterion part, also for reports written before this change. Verdicts and exit
  codes are unchanged.
