- Mutation Flow plants more kinds of Mutants inside its six operators: it
  removes `~`, `-`, and `!`, swaps reduction operators, drops one operand of
  `&&`, `||`, `&`, and `|`, flips `>>`/`>>>` on signed operands, turns
  `$signed` into `$unsigned`, changes enum members and module-level variable
  initializers, swaps neighbouring concatenation operands, and reaches
  `$signed`/`$unsigned` arguments and instance port connections. Existing
  Mutants keep their IDs; the generator version is now 3.
