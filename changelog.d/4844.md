- In physical mode, `cell_count` is now the instance count of the placed
  netlist after timing repair, the netlist the reported area measures, instead
  of the mapped netlist's count. Logical mode is unchanged, and `wire_count`
  stays the mapped netlist's count. Reports gain `cell_count_source`
  (`openroad_post_optimization` or `yosys_mapped`) beside `area_source`.
  Physical cell counts stored by earlier versions, such as Measurement base
  runs and older reports, differ from new ones by a one-time step. A
  `--baseline` run measures both revisions with this version, so its delta
  stays like-for-like.
