- Breaking: a Sandbox set up by hand, such as a Codespace, must now record
  `auth = "visitor"` in `[agent]` to use the credential you sign in with or
  export. A missing `auth` bills the subscription there too, and `booley doctor`
  fails on it. Outside such a Sandbox `visitor` bills the subscription and
  `booley doctor` fails on it, and `booley init` on the host replaces it.
