- Breaking: the Reviewer keeps no review history. `reviewer --verify` and
  `--waive` are removed and refused as unknown options over the CLI and MCP;
  run a new review to check your fixes. An errored review no longer carries
  its filtered and rejected findings into the next run, changed sources no
  longer mark stored review verdicts stale, and overlapping review calls are
  no longer refused. Review receipts no longer carry `tb_policy_digest`, and
  review evidence no longer carries `review_call_order`. The Coverage Analyst
  and the Reviewer neither read nor write a state file. `reviewer-state/`
  directories left by 0.4.0 are no longer read; delete them when you no longer
  need them.
  ([issue #4301](https://github.com/boldaxolotl/booley/issues/4301))
