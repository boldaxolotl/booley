- Synthesis now counts the warnings the Yosys frontends report against source
  lines (`read_verilog`, and `slang` on Yosys 0.69 and 0.70) in `total_warnings`
  under category `frontend`. They never cause `WARN`. The same warning on
  different lines counts per occurrence in `total_warnings` and forms one
  entry in `unique_warnings` and the samples. A loop or multi-driver warning
  keeps its structural classification.
  ([issue #3672](https://github.com/boldaxolotl/booley/issues/3672))
