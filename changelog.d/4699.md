- B-Wave clock findings name a next step. Every sync query on a store with no
  usable clock prints one `no usable clock` warning that says to use
  `--clock PATTERN` or `--async`. The `# clock:` header says whether the clock
  was auto-detected or chosen with `--clock`, and `overview --clock` prints
  the override line. The clock-detection page is gone; its content is in
  `reference/sync-vs-async`.
