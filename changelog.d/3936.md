- Fixed `booley flow mutation --count AUTO` (any case of `auto`) crashing with
  a TypeError: it now behaves like `--count auto`. A `--count` that is not a
  positive integer or `auto` now fails at argument parsing with
  `argument --count: must be a positive integer or 'auto'` (exit 2) instead of
  naming an internal function. `--count 0` is now a parser error instead of a
  run report.
