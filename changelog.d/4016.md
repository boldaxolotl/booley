- A `bugs` review now makes two model calls in parallel: its bug-pattern
  checklist and a new scenario hunt that traces reset, back-to-back, full and
  empty, and abort cases through the RTL. It reports both calls' findings,
  merges findings at the same file, line, and kind, and records which pass found
  each one. The review costs about two reviews, and a failed call fails the
  whole review. Editing a review guide now makes an existing review receipt stale,
  and receipts recorded by earlier releases are stale once.
