- Booley no longer moves a Booley 0.3.x Project's `.booley_project/` to
  `.booley/`. Before upgrading, run `booley init` with Booley 0.4.x in each
  0.3.x Project. This release refuses such a Project (a `.booley_project/` at
  its root, with or without `.booley/`, the journal of an interrupted 0.4.x
  migration, `BOOLEY_PROJECT_DIR` in the environment, or a selection through
  `.booley_project`) with a message to upgrade through Booley 0.4.x:
  `booley init` and `booley doctor` refuse, as does any command that resolves
  that Booley Directory, and `booley init` writes nothing.
  ([#4349](https://github.com/boldaxolotl/booley/issues/4349))
