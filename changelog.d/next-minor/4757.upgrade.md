- **Removed the VaporView waveform viewer.** **Breaking:** Booley no longer
  bundles VaporView or the Viewer Bridge, the Sandbox image no longer installs
  the VaporView extension, and `booley doctor` no longer probes the bridge or
  checks for VaporView. `bwave gui` still resolves its trace and selection and
  reports selector errors, then exits with status 1 and a message that Booley
  has no waveform viewer until the web Dashboard's waveform view ships. The
  text queries, such as `bwave signal`, `wave` and `value`, still work.
  ([#4757](https://github.com/boldaxolotl/Booley/issues/4757))
