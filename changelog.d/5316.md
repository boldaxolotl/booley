- A traced Simulation whose FIFO or post-run waveform conversion failed, but whose declared
  `[flows.sim].trace_files` entry supplied the waveform, now prints one `[trace] note` naming
  `trace.fst.stderr` instead of bwave's `WARNING`/`stderr` lines. Without a waveform those lines
  still print, and the Simulation evidence keeps the failed attempt's bwave output.
