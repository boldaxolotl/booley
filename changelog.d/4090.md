- The Sandbox now mounts the Booley Directory at `/booley`, and `BOOLEY_DIR` inside
  the Sandbox is `/booley`. After you upgrade, `booley init` rebuilds the images
  Booley manages; recreate each Sandbox, as for any upgrade. Booley refuses an
  image you manage yourself until you rebuild it from the current Booley image,
  and refuses a running Sandbox that still uses the previous mount until you
  recreate it. Records, worktrees and root guidance links written under the
  previous mount keep working.
  ([issue #4090](https://github.com/boldaxolotl/booley/issues/4090))
