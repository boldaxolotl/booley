- B-Wave warns, and still exits 0, when a value literal needs more bits than
  its width prefix or than a signal it is compared with, in `find`, `sample`,
  `distance`, `stuck` and `--virtual`, naming the literal as typed. A hex
  literal such as `'h100` no longer also matches the value its digits spell in
  binary. The radix-pitfalls troubleshooting page is removed.
