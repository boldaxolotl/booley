- `booley flow sim --coverage` now reports the coverage hook error (for
  example `COV_WRITE_HOOK_MISSING` when a custom `main` never calls
  `booley_coverage_write()`) instead of `COV_RAW_FILE_MISSING` or
  `COV_RAW_FILE_STALE` when a hook was not called correctly and the test left
  no fresh native database.
