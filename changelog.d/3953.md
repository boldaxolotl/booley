- `booley measure [<sha>]` measures a commit (`HEAD` by default) against
  `metrics.toml` in a clean checkout and stores the Measurement Run. `--base`
  picks the Base, `--all` evaluates every table after a failure, and `--json`
  prints the report as JSON. It exits 0 when every enforced bound is met, 1 when
  one is unmet, and 2 when a Flow errored, nothing is in scope or the
  measurement could not run.
